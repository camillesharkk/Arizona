## First commission

Use the six-year term process and current applicant qualifications in 32-3-120. The lawful-presence and military-spouse/dependent alternatives are expressly included. Complete the current prescribed application, oath, education/exam certification and sixty-dollar fee. Current form requires hard-copy mail or delivery, check or money order and another commissioned notary for Part IV. Bond and E&O are voluntary and are not filed with county or state. The appointment letter and certificate supply the new stamp information.

The opened state form has twenty true-or-false statements with at least fourteen correct required. No numerical timer is established. An approved provider education/exam completion certificate allows skipping Part III. The optional alternative does not remove the remaining application or oath requirements.

## Renewal

Education and examination apply to each new and renewed term. The Chapter 6 state-route presentation requirement says every term, and the official directory identifies approved alternative education/exam entities. The six-year renewed commission requires a new physical or electronic stamp. A name-change certificate does not start a new term, as the January 2026 name-change instructions expressly state.

## Remote and border rules

Prior notice identifies intended technology before an initial electronic-record or remote-person act. RON is the electronic-record route and uses the statutory single recorded session and quality/same-record determinations. RIN is expressly limited to a tangible-record acknowledgment with prior signing, dating and submission of the paper, followed by the remote acknowledgment. It is not taught as a universal ink procedure for jurats.

All remote communication is recorded audiovisually in full under 32-3-118(a). The statutory sole-possession and official access conditions apply. Remote certificates identify use of technology and name the actual system under Chapter 6. Applicable system or rule requirements can require additional identity methods, but no unverified universal KBA question count, time or passing specification is introduced. Overseas principals meet both foreign-prohibition and listed U.S.-nexus conditions.

The Wyoming bordering-state authority in 32-3-104(b) is conditional on the bordering state's recognition. This pack does not assert any particular neighboring state currently recognizes that authority. It teaches the condition and Wyoming-law adherence, with actual state/county venue. Recognition of another state's officer's act under 32-3-106 is a separate issue and does not grant a Wyoming commission.

## Other distinctions preserved

- Official-copy prohibition has the express employment exception for the public entity issuing or holding the original in 32-3-123(a)(iv). The education slides simplify the general restriction, while the pack preserves the statutory exception and the separate accurate-copy determination.
- Physical-inability witness-signing uses one unaffected witness; officer-signing uses two unaffected witnesses. Both have their own identifying notation, witness ID and journal participation duties.
- Journals chronicle every act on either medium and cannot be shared by officers. Original employer surrender at termination needs state approval. The employer may retain the stated employment-scope copy.
- No unsupported mandatory numerical journal or recording retention period is created. The presentation recommends journal retention for the term, while statute controls lawful surrender/destruction and rules allow additional retention requirements. The recommendation is not described as automatic destruction permission.
- Certificate text correction, later proper stamp impression, and written authorization to a third party have different conditions in 32-3-114(h)-(k). The original officer retains correction authority and the prescribed copies and notation are preserved.
- Travel fee agreement precedes travel, while technology total agreement precedes the act. Both have separate-charge explanations. The cancellation rule distinguishes principal short-notice cancellation/lawful refusal from officer cancellation without completion. Innocent proofing failure is included in technology-fee nonrefund discretion.
