# Illinois has temporarily waived the electronic notary application fee

The Illinois Study Guide now explains the temporary fee waiver for an electronic notary commission. Illinois still charges $15 for a traditional commission application. If you already hold a traditional commission and apply only to add electronic authority, the state filing fee is waived during the program. Training and bond requirements still apply.

The law took effect July 24, 2026, and the Secretary of State reports implementing the waiver July 27. The waiver is scheduled to end July 24, 2028. When you study Illinois fees, separate what an applicant pays to the state from what a commissioned notary may charge for an act.
