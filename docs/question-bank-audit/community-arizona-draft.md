# Arizona practice update on deed acknowledgments

We updated AZ-013 and the acknowledgment chapter of our [Arizona Study Guide](https://arizonanotaryprep.com/arizona-notary-study-guide/#acknowledgments) to cover the journal requirement for a deed that was signed before the signer arrived.

Since September 12, 2026, Arizona law has required a journal thumbprint for covered deeds, other documents affecting real property, and powers of attorney, subject to statutory exceptions. For an in-person deed acknowledgment, start with the right thumb. If it is unavailable, use the left thumb or an available finger and note that in the journal. If the signer is physically unable to provide either, record the reason. The law exempts a trustee’s deed resulting from foreclosure and a deed of release and reconveyance.

A signer can still acknowledge a signature they made earlier. For practice, remember that the date on the notarial certificate is the date you perform the acknowledgment. Then check whether the document triggers the journal thumbprint rule.

[Arizona Laws 2026, chapter 31, § 6](https://www.azleg.gov/legtext/57leg/2r/laws/0031.htm)
