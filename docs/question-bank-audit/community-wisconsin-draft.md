# Wisconsin online filing and remote notarization

Wisconsin’s Department of Financial Institutions now directs four-year notary applicants and renewing notaries to its Wisconsin Integrated Management System, or WIMS. The state had scheduled the portal transition for November 16, 2026, at 7 a.m. Central. Open WIMS through DFI’s [Notary File Online](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/ConsumerServices/NotaryPublic/NotaryFileOnline.aspx) page. The exam and the applicable oath and bond requirements are still separate steps.

We updated the Wisconsin commissioning guide and filing card and replaced WI-056 with WI-204 so the practice question teaches the current filing route. We also corrected WI-127 and WI-128 on remote platform approval. The notary must notify DFI, upload their digital seal and signature, and wait for DFI approval. Approved providers have their own reporting duty for newly authorized users.

Review the [DFI renewal FAQ](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/ConsumerServices/NotaryPublic/NotaryFAQ.aspx) and [DFI’s current remote registration instructions](https://dfi.wi.gov/Pages/ConsumerServices/NotaryPublic/RemoteOnlineNotary.aspx) before filing.
