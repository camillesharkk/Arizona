# California notary fees change January 1, 2027

California will raise several notary fee limits on January 1, 2027. The maximum for a standard acknowledgment increases from $15 to $20 per signature. The new limits also apply to jurats and certified copies of powers of attorney. Deposition fees have their own limits.

The date in the scenario matters. CA-079 uses a November 2026 deed, so the current $15 rate still applies. For practice scenarios dated January 1, 2027 or later, use the new schedule and check whether an exception applies.
