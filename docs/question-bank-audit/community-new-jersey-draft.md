# New Jersey updated its official notary exam questions in September

New Jersey’s Division of Revenue and Enterprise Services added a new set of questions to the official notary exam on September 24, 2026. The state revised its Notary Public Manual on September 14 to reflect continuing-education requirements for renewal.

Our New Jersey study guide now explains those changes and points to the current state materials. NJ-003 also clarifies who uses the examination route after a lapse of more than 30 days. First-time nonattorney applicants should read the current manual and complete the state’s video training. Active renewers can use the state’s current continuing-education instructions.

Our original practice questions are based on public rules, the state manual and the exam instructions. The live exam uses an access key.
