## First commission

Use the ordinary online test and the current commission process. The current process requires a mandatory background check and instructs applicants to continue with bond and oath without waiting for a separate result notification. A five-thousand-dollar ordinary surety bond, constitutional oath and approval are required before effectiveness. The process specifies no Final Submit button after document uploads. The pack does not alter baseline exam specifications or claim newly verified question counts, time limits or passing scores.

## Renewal

An expired commission cannot support more acts. A new application demonstrating compliance is required. An ordinary commission received on or after May 6, 2026, including renewal, brings the physical-journal requirement for nonremote acts subject to the actual employment exceptions. Prior commissioning experience does not preserve the old ordinary optional-journal rule for the new commission.

## RON

The statute permits an ordinary commissioned person or applicant to apply for certification, while the operational RON page states an existing ordinary commission as the prerequisite to its published process. The content follows that published operational sequence. Remote approval is separate and reapplied for upon ordinary renewal. Total bond coverage is ten thousand dollars. The notary must be physically in Utah. Remote identity, recording, electronic journal, tamper evidence, black seal and vendor-custody restrictions are distinct from ordinary commissioning requirements.

## 2026 journal and numbering reconciliation

The current chapter renumbered several definitions. The pack uses 46-1-2(4) for copy certification, (12) for jurat, (19) for oath or affirmation, (22) for personally knows and (27) for signature witnessing. Copy-certificate form is 46-1-6.5(4). The employment journal exception is 46-1-13(4). New-cohort detailed entries come from 46-1-14.1, while 46-1-14 expressly addresses the older cohort. The current 46-1-13(2) cross-reference still prints 46-1-14, so the content uses the expressly applicable 46-1-14.1 text and the office supplement rather than importing the sunsetting details. All secondary-authentication examples refer to identity verification and are not characterized as commissioning exams.

The ten-year period runs from the last notarial act in the journal, continues after leaving office and includes destruction duties after the period. The exact employer exceptions, ordinary specific-entry access and protected biometric/ID-image release rules are explained separately. No ordinary private employer ownership assumption is imported into the attorney, law-firm or title-agency exception.
