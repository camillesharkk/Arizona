## Current instructions and older guide passages

The served guide retains a 2024 edition heading and some transition language. The active-notary training omission described there ended on January 1, 2025. The present content follows the current universal Basics requirement. The current RON page supplies the current submission route and samples. The package does not publish an older RON email address or claim that ordinary commissioning by itself permits RON.

The guide distinguishes maintaining a journal from entry requirements. Its detailed journal chapter requires ordinary acknowledgment and signature-witnessing entries and strongly recommends recording other ordinary acts. That distinction is retained. The current RON FAQ separately requires an electronic journal for remote acts. IPEN can use the permitted journal media described by the detailed FAQ. The package avoids generalizing the ordinary-paper choice to RON.

The RON identity quiz specifications in OAR 160-100-0805 describe participant authentication. They do not establish specifications for the Oregon commission examination. The existing unknown ordinary examination count, duration and numerical threshold remain unchanged in the base metadata.
