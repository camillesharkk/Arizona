## First commission, renewal and remote distinctions

Ordinary first qualification follows the current July 2026 electronic-application guide followed by county oath recording and physical state return. Older instructions to mail the initial application were not carried into the new content. The recorded oath is still physically returned and state processing activates the commission.

The July 2026 guide permits renewal of expired records or records within sixty days of expiration. Older FAQ restrictions after expiration remain a documented conflict and are not reintroduced. New renewal bond/exam evidence and the oath process remain separate from the initial application's old documents.

Dedicated approved RON guidance controls over obsolete broad statements that all remote acts are unavailable. In-person electronic work retains physical proximity when approved RON technology is absent. Electronic qualification and two-year refresher training are distinct from ordinary first/renewal assessment. No unknown ordinary exam time limit or electronic test question count is invented.

## Rule-specific boundaries

The ordinary traditional register is described as recommended. Suggested fields and evidence value are not converted into invented statutory mandates. A journal does not cure a missing act or replace the remote recording. The handbook's corporate employee allowance is kept subject to absence of personal involvement and representation in the instrument. Financial conflicts and advertising disclaimers retain the handbook's scope.

Copy certification screens vital/public records and specified agency-issued records and preserves the handbook's explicit passport and driver's-license allowances. Statements concern reproduction of an original, without turning a copy certificate into a legal-validity opinion. Accessible-signature content distinguishes the mark procedure's one disinterested witness from directed third-party signing with two disinterested witnesses.
