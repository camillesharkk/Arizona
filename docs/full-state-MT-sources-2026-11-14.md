## Ordinary first commission

SOS requires four hours of approved education within the prior twelve months and its examination. The examination page states an 80% passing score, three attempts followed by a three-month waiting period, and a passing result within six months before application. No verified ordinary exam question count or duration is asserted. Education and exam completion precede the online application. The $25,000 assurance and notarized oath, exact application/bond name agreement, stated filing window, fee and statutory qualifications are supported by the SOS application page and MCA 1-5-619. No private course certificate is portrayed as a commission.

## Renewal

The SOS ordinary renewal route also requires the exam. Education may be four approved hours in the previous twelve months or the stated two hours in each of the preceding three years. Portal timing is described separately from authority to act. The thirty-day post-expiration application window does not extend an expired commission. The current commission term is four years under MCA 1-5-619.

## Electronic and remote services

MCA 1-5-615 and the SOS technology page govern notification, approved course and examination evidence before first technology-based acts. IPEN retains physical appearance. RON uses electronic records with remote appearance. SOS describes remote paper acknowledgment with the actual wet-signed record delivered to the notary. MCA 1-5-603 supplies identity, same-record, recording-session, communication quality, secure authentication, international nexus and known-foreign-prohibition conditions. The notary remains physically in Montana. These technology qualifications are additional to an ordinary commission.

## Expanded substantive coverage

- MCA 1-5-603 supports distinct identity paths, representative capacity, copy certification, certification of fact, signature witnessing and oath determinations.
- MCA 1-5-609 supports contemporaneous certificates and journal-supported later corrections. Only the original officer makes or authorizes a correction. Written third-party authorization and retained copies are addressed. An existing stamp image itself cannot be edited.
- MCA 1-5-616 and 617 support signature consistency, stamp content, sole ownership, security, loss reporting and disabling devices when authority ends.
- MCA 1-5-618 supports transaction entries, excluded sensitive identifiers, employer copies, original control, separate public journal and audiovisual access conditions, reporting and retention. The ordinary journal retention trigger is the last chronicled act. Recording retention runs from the recording date. Ordinary recording access requires a reasonable purpose directly relating to the notarization. Ordinary journal access uses the statute's criminal/illegal-purpose restriction.
- MCA 1-5-621 and 622 support commission integrity, reporting and mandatory refusal. MCA 1-5-623 supports directed signing for physical inability while preserving capacity and voluntary-execution requirements.
- MCA 1-5-625 supports self-interest, representative signer conflicts, official-record copy restrictions and unauthorized legal/immigration services. The public-entity employment exception is kept explicit.
- MCA 1-5-626 supports the $10 maximum per listed act, additional-charge disclosure and advance agreement, and the statutory IRS-mileage travel ceiling. No current numerical IRS rate is invented.
- MCA 1-5-629 locates venue at the officer's actual physical place. MCA 1-5-632 supports record protection and unauthorized certificate-change prohibitions.
- The SOS states that the former individual vehicle-transfer acknowledgment requirement ended October 1, 2025. Current MCA 61-3-220 retains title assignment/authorization, delivery, application and applicable deadline/penalty provisions. The content separates those vehicle obligations from the removed notarization requirement.
