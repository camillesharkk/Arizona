## First commission and renewal

Ordinary four-year applicants complete the required final, including renewals. Readings and chapter quizzes may be skipped by a knowledgeable applicant, while the final remains required. Current published final has thirty questions and ninety-percent passage, one sitting and no save/resume. It gives no numeric time limit. Save the passing evidence because the instructions say the system does not store the score or regenerate it. Assessment is taken no more than one year before commission or renewal.

Follow the current filing instructions for the oath before a current notarial officer, separate specific five-hundred-dollar bond, and samples of active seals and signatures. Notice that the commission has issued precedes acts. A permanent attorney commission has separate good-standing evidence, no ordinary bond and conditions tied to Wisconsin law licensure. Attorney status alone does not appoint the person as notary.

## RON and estate planning

No further state commission or license is required for Wisconsin remote work according to the notary guidance. Ordinary RON nevertheless requires approved technology, provider authorization and training, same-record confirmation, applicable proofing, tamper evidence, communication-technology certificate language, audiovisual recording and at least seven-year retention. The notary remains physically in Wisconsin and responsible when delegating storage. The personal-knowledge exception is an identity-proofing exception and does not erase recording or retention.

Part 3 estate-planning remote acts use the materially different 140.147 process. A Wisconsin attorney in good standing supervises and may also serve as commissioned notary. Both notary and remote individual are physically in Wisconsin. The estate procedure has its own audiovisual requirements and does not universally require a Part 2C approved platform. Follow document indications, declarations, visible execution, the listed original/copy options, attorney retention and the affidavit of compliance. Limited financial powers for real-estate transactions are specifically distinguished from the broader estate power-of-attorney list. No generic provider workflow is presented as covering all documents.
