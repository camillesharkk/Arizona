## First application: established workflow and materials

The May 2026 FAQ describes Access IN login, Applicant Hub for applications, and Learner Hub for assigned education and active commission information. An application has personal information, residency, background, bond, signature, and payment sections. The applicant submits the application first. After application approval, an email announces that the assigned education is ready in Learner Hub. Application approval that merely unlocks education must not be described as authority to notarize.

Prepare a valid ISP Criminal History report dated within six months; a bond of at least $25,000 for eight years with the exact applicant name as principal; and an ink signature on a clean white background matching the application name. Nonresidents primarily employed in Indiana provide employer details and Employer Consent. The current FAQ permits bypassing or disputing automated document validation; staff review can still follow. Passing an automated check is not a grant of commission.

The official 2024 fee publication lists a $75 initial application charge. The state portal's live payment screen was not accessed, and the 2026 FAQ expressly publishes $75 for renewal rather than separately restating the initial fee. Keep the first-application fee attributed to the dated publication and tell applicants to check the amount shown before paying. Bond premiums, supplies, and background-report charges are separate; no unsupported present-day total is offered.

## Eligibility and official result

The retrieved official guide p. 8 requires age 18 or older, U.S. citizenship or permanent legal residence, Indiana residence or primary Indiana employment, and absence of disqualifying conduct. Its conduct discussion and IC 33-42-13 require individual review; the pack must not promise that every conviction either always qualifies or always disqualifies. The statutory educational and examination qualifications were crosschecked in the 2025 mirror of IC 33-42-12-1 and -2.

The 2026 FAQ publishes 30 exam questions and a pass mark of 80%, meaning 24 correct. Failed attempts require a 24-hour wait; attempts are unlimited. The Learner Hub tracks course and exam status. Approved commission holders access the official certificate through My Commission. The FAQ does not establish a requirement to buy a private certificate, submit a separate commercial practice score, furnish school grades, or obtain a vendor credential. Absence of such a requirement in the retrieved material is not a claim that staff can never request clarification of an application.

The live Applicant Hub was not entered or submitted. No applicant's eligibility, education assignment, score, commission issue, effective date, or certificate download was tested. No application fees were paid and no personal documents were uploaded.

## Ordinary-law crosschecks and unresolved currency

Because official current Code text could not be retrieved, the following [2025 Indiana Code mirror](https://law.justia.com/codes/indiana/title-33/article-42/) sections were read as corroboration, not mislabeled official 2026 verification:

- IC 33-42-9-2: identity/signature determination and accurate, full, true copies.
- IC 33-42-9-4: personal knowledge; allowed government credentials no more than three years expired; credible witness; additional identification.
- IC 33-42-9-5: competence, knowing/voluntary execution, and refusal subject to law.
- IC 33-42-9-6: physical inability, designated signer, and labeling the designee/principal plus intent language.
- IC 33-42-9-12: contemporaneous certificate, date/signature/venue/title/commission particulars, association with the record, no presigning, subsequent corrections, and no alteration of a seal impression.
- IC 33-42-10-2 and -3: seal content and reproducibility; sole device use; prompt lost/stolen notice; preventing further use when authority ends.
- IC 33-42-12-1 and -2: qualifications, assurance, term, statewide authority without immunity, education and exam.
- IC 33-42-13-3: prohibited acts, direct-benefit conflicts, reading for blind acknowledgers, language understanding, legal/immigration services, notario advertising, and withholding originals.
- IC 33-42-14-1: listed ordinary fees are per signature, advance display, and private-agreement/travel provisions. The simplified $10-per-act presentation wording is not used to erase the statutory detail.

Official indexed guide p. 19 distinguishes issuer-certified official records from a person's true-copy affidavit. The notary does not become a birth-record, school, court, or other issuer merely by stamping a photocopy. Direct-copy instruction is kept subject to this boundary.

The seal list in the 2025 official presentation and the 2025 mirror of IC 33-42-10-2 reflect P.L.130-2024: the word seal is not itself a mandatory seal element. Older local pages still reproduce the old list.

2026 SB253 search results are an introduced proposal, not an enacted-law source. Proposed identity-proofing language and a proposed stamping-device seller proof-retention rule are excluded. The official bill page also rendered a shell; no claim of a complete enacted-laws audit is made. Current Code and IAC currency remain an explicit legal-review gap before public release.
