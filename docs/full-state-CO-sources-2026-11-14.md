## Ordinary first and renewal

## Act and identity boundaries

Credible witnesses require their own knowledge, appearance and sworn written verification. Acknowledgment of an earlier or fax-copied signature is distinguished from witnessing execution. Oath/affirmation, certificate date, venue, correction roles and blank-field instructions follow their specific FAQ answers. ## Journal and fee exceptions

Employer-record exception requires all journal information in the retained record. It is separate from leaving a completed journal with the employer. Rules 4.1–4.3 govern that custody route, thirty-day notice and continuing security/access duties. Ordinary journal fields, separate signer entries, oral oaths and remote electronic format are preserved.

Current official fee FAQ lists $15 ordinary per document and $25 electronic/remote. Older lower limits are excluded. Fee documentation distinguishes title-company employees from independent contractors, entirely free service from zero notarial price with charged associated services, and advance information from written delivery timing. The detailed FAQ's separate signing-service qualifications are acknowledged wherever the associated-charge general rule is discussed.
