## Capability and wording decision

Texas belongs in the `courseQuiz` group. Public wording uses Practice Questions and Course Review. Applications and reappointment applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026 use the SOS-established and offered education courses and their assessment. A private preparation result supplies study feedback. Official education is completed through the SOS course.

## Verified first traditional appointment pathway

1. Establish ordinary eligibility under Government Code §406.004, including Texas residency, age eighteen and the applicable conviction provisions.
2. Create or use SOSPortal access. A previously commissioned person finds the existing profile before education.
3. Pay the separate course fee and complete the SOS traditional education requirement before applying. Watch the required videos and submit the official assessment. Preserve the confirmation and completion/payment proof.
4. Obtain proof of the ordinary ten-thousand-dollar surety bond from the qualified Texas surety agency. The statutory bond and its premium are different amounts.
5. Submit the prescribed application, officer statement, bond proof and fees through the official portal. Ordinary filing components under §406.007 total twenty-one dollars. Portal charges and education are separate payments.
6. On approval, download and review the commission, execute the official oath or affirmation before another authorized officer, and establish the compliant seal and records before duties.

Sources are the official notary entry, traditional portal guide and Government Code §§406.004–406.010.

## Renewal and online pathways

Reappointment requires the SOS continuing education before applying under §87.9. The ordinary reappointment application window opens ninety days before expiration under §406.011. A pending application does not extend an expired term. The official existing-profile workflow remains relevant to returning notaries.

The statutory online application provision includes a traditional notary or a traditional applicant while incorporating Subchapter A qualifications. The SOS operational Getting Started page requires a current Texas traditional commission for its online application pathway. Complete the applicable separate online education, acquire the prescribed electronic seal and compliant digital certificate, and submit the online application. The published online application fee is fifty dollars plus current portal payment charges.

The SOS reminder directs renewal of the traditional commission and receipt of its certificate before online renewal. Online authority expires with traditional authority. Compliant secure two-way audio-video, lawful identity verification, certificates, exclusive control, records and backups remain necessary after application approval.

## Dates and detailed legal decisions

- SB693 amended qualification, continuing education, appearance and record provisions effective September 1, 2025. The adopted education implementation applies to applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026.
- Adopted §§87.5,87.8 and 87.9 set twenty dollars per initial or continuing course taking, nonrefundable. The course is no more than two hours, with at least seventy percent correct, a ninety-day fee-receipt-to-course deadline and at most three completions in three months.
- Adopted §§87.13 and 87.14 require completion proof within the one year preceding application submission, with the relevant course-fee payment proof. This is separate from the ninety-day deadline for taking the course after fee receipt. The adoption response also identifies §87.20 for state-employee application proof.
- The adopted passing percentage supersedes the eighty-percent figure in the proposal. The public portal guides give thirty minutes for the assessment and warn that failing to select final Submit produces a zero score and loss of the attempt.
- Current §406.014(g) requires keeping its subsection (a) records until the tenth anniversary of notarization. The online electronic-record provision in §406.108(c) states at least five years for its record. Content identifies both and preserves applicable duties together.
- Current §121.005(a)(1), amended by HB4202 effective June 20, 2025, permits identifying a credible witness with the stated current federal or state document bearing photo and signature as an alternative to personal knowledge. The witness oath remains required.
- The foreign-passport acknowledgment route in §121.005(a)(3) expressly concerns a deed or other instrument relating to residential real estate.
- Current §406.0091 defines traditional and online appearance and creates the knowing absent-person offense, including its real-property enhancement and specified affirmative defense.
- Current §406.1103 includes tangible-document online notarization safeguards. Its specified acknowledgment procedure includes recorded signing/declaration, sending by day three and receipt by day ten. Subsection (e) preserves another procedure meeting the same-document requirement.
- Section 406.010(f) has the state-officer/employee bond exception. Published §87.12 provides its narrow qualified-escrow-officer residency exception for New Mexico, Oklahoma, Arkansas and Louisiana. Ordinary-residency questions are explicitly framed around the ordinary applicant.
- Current §406.013 includes the identifying number on the seal. Some SOS educational text reproduces older abbreviated seal wording. The current codified statute supplies the complete elements used in this content.
